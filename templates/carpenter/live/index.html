What We Build

Our
Services

Whether you need a single statement piece or a complete home transformation, every project receives the same level of care — meticulous, unhurried, and built to outlast trends.

i
Custom Furniture

Dining tables, bed frames, bookshelves, and display cabinets made to your exact dimensions, timber choice, and finish. One of a kind, forever.

ii
Fitted Kitchens

Hand-crafted kitchen units, island benches, and pantry storage. We design around your space to maximise every centimetre with beauty and function.

iii
Built-in Wardrobes

Floor-to-ceiling fitted wardrobes and walk-in closet systems. Custom compartments, soft-close rails, and seamless finishes that look built into the architecture.

iv
Decks & Pergolas

Hardwood and treated pine outdoor structures that turn your garden into a living space. Engineered for the KZN climate, beautiful for life.

v
Doors & Frames

Solid timber doors, architraves, and skirting boards. We hang, fit, and finish — restoring character to older homes or adding warmth to new builds.

vi
Wood Restoration

Stripping, sanding, and refinishing antique and worn timber pieces. We restore their original warmth without losing the patina that makes them irreplaceable.

About the Studio

The Art of
True Craft

We're a small, dedicated joinery studio based in Durban, KZN. Not a factory, not a franchise — just master craftspeople who believe furniture should be made the right way, using real wood, real joinery, and real care.

Every piece starts as a conversation. We listen to how you live, sketch together, source the timber, and build from scratch in our workshop. No shortcuts, no flat-pack. Just wood, tools, and decades of knowing what we're doing.

🌲
Sustainably Sourced Timber

We work with responsible South African suppliers — local species where possible, FSC-certified imports when needed.

📐
Measured to the Millimetre

Everything is built to precise specifications. We take four measurements where others take two — because a perfect fit is non-negotiable.

🤝
Fixed Price, No Surprises

We quote thoroughly upfront. If something changes, we discuss it with you before spending a cent more. Always.

🛡️
Guaranteed Craftsmanship

All our work carries a 5-year structural guarantee. If a joint fails or a finish deteriorates prematurely, we make it right.

How It Works

The
Process

From first conversation to final delivery, every step is thoughtful, transparent, and focused on producing something you'll treasure for decades.

I
Consultation

We visit your space, discuss your vision, lifestyle, and budget. No obligation — just a good conversation about what's possible.

II
Design & Quote

We sketch the concept, select materials, and provide a fully detailed fixed quote. You see exactly what you're getting before we start.

III
Build in Workshop

Work begins in our Durban workshop. We share progress photos throughout the build. Precision, patience, and no rushing.

IV
Deliver & Install

We deliver, install, and only leave when everything is perfectly in place and you're completely satisfied. Then we disappear quietly.
