A web design studio that actually gives a damn about the businesses it builds for — started in 2024 by a developer who got tired of watching great local businesses lose to bad websites.

// 001 — Our Story

IndieWebStudios started with a simple observation: the best businesses in Durban — the ones with real skill, real service, real heart — were invisible online. Not because they didn't deserve to be found. Because the web had left them behind.

Big agencies weren't interested. DIY builders produced embarrassing results. And the gap between "we have a website" and "our website actually works for us" had never been wider.

So in 2024, we opened our doors with one purpose: build world-class websites for the businesses that deserve them most. No fluff. No agency overhead. Just craft.
